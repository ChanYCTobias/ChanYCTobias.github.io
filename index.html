Simulation research project

RL Manipulation Pick-and-Place

Conference paper and implementation references for Isaac Lab manipulation experiments under privileged-pose corruption. The project focuses on whether policies can remain usable when pose channels become unreliable at deployment time.

  • Benchmarks PPO behavior under hard missing, freeze, noise-and-drift, and delayed pose inputs.
  • Tests whether modality dropout improves resilience more reliably than simply injecting extra sensing signals.
  • Uses a pick-and-place task as a controlled environment for robustness analysis and failure characterization.

Conference draft

Modality Dropout in Privileged-Pose Manipulation (RL Paper)

This paper evaluates PPO policies under realistic pose outages and asks whether dropout-aware training can preserve manipulation performance when privileged state inputs become unreliable.

  • Studies failure modes such as hard missing measurements, frozen pose streams, noise with drift, and delayed inputs.
  • Compares dropout-aware training against the simpler strategy of adding more sensing modalities.
  • Frames the results around robustness rather than best-case nominal performance.

Accepted conference paper

LeCal: Latency-Aware Dataset Curation and Alignment

LeCal is a practical post-hoc calibration workflow for LeRobot teleoperation datasets. It estimates action-to-state delay, surfaces confidence diagnostics, and exports alignment artifacts for downstream model training.

  • Measures latency between operator action streams and observed robot state transitions.
  • Includes confidence diagnostics and camera offset checks so dataset issues are visible rather than hidden.
  • Produces reusable alignment outputs that can be carried directly into training workflows.

Finite-element materials study

Reliability-Aware FEM Design of Functionally Graded Plate Metamaterials

Finite-element research on functionally graded perforated plate metamaterials. The study looks at how graded perforation patterns redistribute stress and stiffness, then evaluates that design space through a reliability-aware lens.

  • Uses FEM to compare mechanically lighter perforated layouts against structural robustness requirements.
  • Focuses on graded metamaterial plates where geometry strongly shapes load paths and stress concentrations.
  • Frames the contribution as a design tradeoff between weight reduction, stiffness distribution, and reliability.
