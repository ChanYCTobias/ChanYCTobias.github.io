Working draft

Crosswind: A Closed-Form Equivariant Navigation Reflex under Bounded Observation Delay

Tobias Chan · 28 August 2026

Draft Multi-robot control Observation delay Safety certificates

Pairwise disc robots under sampled double-integrator dynamics, bounded sensing error, and bounded asynchronous observation age — with no communication and no online optimizer. The controller is an explicit equivariant map: goal PD, a radial push, and a left-chiral tangent, with declared age and error tubes subtracted from clearance.

On a named transit class, the draft reports Lipschitz delay-on safety and finite-horizon ranking certificates, then a 2×2 comparison against an HOCBF quadratic-program filter. Tubes are the causal safety ingredient; the closed-form law with tubes is the only cell that stands. The manuscript is scoped: it is not a deadlock-free theorem and not a claim that HOCBF is dominated.
